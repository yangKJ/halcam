Before you contact us

  • Include your iPhone or iPad model, system version, and HAL version.
  • Describe what you did, including the camera, film, and capture mode.
  • Add a screenshot or recording if helpful, after checking it for private content.

Do not send Apple Account passwords, payment card numbers, or unrelated private photos. See the Privacy Policy for how support email is handled.

The camera view is black. What should I try?

Check HAL's camera permission in system settings, then return to the camera. Make sure another app is not using the camera and check available storage. If the view remains black, contact support with the device, system version, and steps. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step because local works not saved elsewhere may be lost.

Why is a work in HAL missing from system Photos?

The HAL library and system Photos store content separately. Choose save or share from a work and allow HAL to add photos. Deleting a work in HAL does not automatically delete a copy already saved to system Photos.

Why is a camera or media type unavailable?

Available cameras, accessories, capture modes, and import formats depend on the current app and your device. Use combinations actually enabled in the app. When reporting a failure, include the camera, film, media type, and steps.

How do I restore HAL Pro?

Use the Apple Account used for purchase and choose Restore Purchases on the HAL Pro page. Apple must verify a valid transaction. Access may be unavailable if a product has not launched or a transaction was refunded or revoked. Do not delete local works to restore a purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Apple manages subscriptions. View or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting HAL does not cancel a subscription. Request a refund through Apple Report a Problem. Apple determines eligibility under its rules.

What should I do before deleting the app?

Save important photos, videos, and works to system Photos or another backup first. HAL does not offer developer-operated photo cloud sync. Content not saved elsewhere may be unrecoverable after removing the app or its data.

Are my photos uploaded?

HAL focuses on local capture, processing, and work management. Photos and videos are not automatically uploaded to developer servers. Sharing, sending support email, and system retrieval from iCloud are separate actions you choose. See the Privacy Policy.

When can I download HAL?

HAL is preparing to launch. Download links and final supported features will be announced at release. There is currently no public download or test invitation link.
