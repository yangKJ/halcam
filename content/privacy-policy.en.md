# HAL Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 17, 2026

## Scope and contact

This policy applies to the HAL camera app and the website and support channels maintained by the HAL developer. Privacy and support: [yangkj310@gmail.com](mailto:yangkj310@gmail.com).

HAL focuses on capture and image processing on your device. It does not require a HAL account, offer developer-operated photo cloud sync, or include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or external crash-reporting SDKs. Your photos, videos, location, and edits are not automatically uploaded to developer servers.

## Camera and microphone

HAL accesses the camera only with permission when you use capture features for photos, Live Photos, or videos. Recording content with sound requires microphone permission. Change permissions in system settings or turn off audio in supported capture modes. Denying permission affects features that depend on it.

## Photos, imports, and sharing

Photos, videos, and Live Photos you choose are read and processed on your device and stored in the local HAL library as needed. The system photo picker provides selected content. When you browse HAL's built-in album browser, the app requests Photos read permission and reads album information and thumbnails within the limited or full access you grant. Original resources are retrieved after you select an item for import. Change or revoke Photos permission in system settings.

When you choose to save, HAL requests permission to add results to system Photos. Sharing through the system share sheet sends selected content to your chosen recipient, which handles it under its own rules. If an original is stored only in iCloud, the system may retrieve it from Apple under your settings. This is not an upload to the HAL developer.

## Location and motion

HAL requests location permission only when you enable saving capture locations, to associate location with local works and saved photos. Turning it off stops new updates. HAL does not upload location to developer servers. Saved or shared photos may retain location metadata; manage metadata and recipients as needed.

The viewfinder level uses device motion to indicate tilt. Updates stop when the relevant viewfinder state ends. Motion history is not stored.

## Local storage, retention, and deletion

HAL stores original media, processed versions, film and editing parameters, work records, personal films, preferences, and necessary caches on your device for capture, development, and recovery. Use the app's deletion and storage controls to remove corresponding content.

Local content generally remains until you delete it or remove app data through the system. Deleting works in HAL or removing the app does not automatically delete copies saved to system Photos or shared elsewhere. Those copies are managed by the relevant system or recipient. System backups and iCloud retention depend on your settings and Apple's policies.

## In-app purchases

HAL Pro is planned to offer an annual subscription and a one-time lifetime unlock. Availability, price, currency, offers, and benefits are determined by the app's descriptions and Apple's purchase confirmation. This policy does not promise that a product is already available.

Apple StoreKit and related services handle purchases, verification, restoration, renewals, and refunds. HAL reads Apple-verified transactions and entitlements to determine feature access. It does not receive payment card numbers, bank account details, or Apple Account passwords. Apple's processing is governed by its own privacy policy.

## Support email

The developer receives your email address, message, and materials you choose to attach only when you contact support, to respond to, investigate, and resolve your request. Do not send Apple Account passwords, payment card information, or unrelated private photos.

Support materials are retained as needed for the request, necessary security review, and applicable legal obligations, then removed according to normal email and backup cleanup cycles. Request access, correction, or deletion of materials you voluntarily provided by emailing support. Requests are verified and handled under applicable law.

## Website and third-party services

HAL public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process IP addresses and network information needed to provide and secure its services under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). HAL pages do not add analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking scripts.

Apple, GitHub, email providers, and recipients you select handle information independently under their own policies. This policy does not replace their privacy notices.

## Your controls

- Change camera, microphone, Photos, and location permissions in system settings.
- Turn off saving location or avoid the corresponding feature.
- Delete works and local content in HAL, and manage system Photos and external copies separately.
- Cancel imports or sharing and choose trusted recipients.
- Manage purchases and subscriptions through Apple, and contact support about materials you voluntarily provided.

## Children and policy updates

HAL is not designed to collect children's personal information and does not offer a public community or interactions with strangers. Guardians should help children manage captured content, permissions, and sharing. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information through support.

We will update this policy and applicable App Store privacy information when functionality or data practices change, with notice or consent where required. This policy does not pre-authorize future accounts, cloud sync, or remote image processing.
